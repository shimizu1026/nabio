Business 事業内容

Our company is commercializing the results of scientific research on natto.
Bacteria that grow natto not only produce nattokinase, but also a variety of other substances that are beneficial to health.
Our company manufactures and sells products that utilize the beneficial substances produced by natto bacteria.
Our sales are primarily in Asia, but are also expanding worldwide
